# ShopEase image sources

The active branded catalog uses product photographs and product names from the [SM Stationery school collection](https://smstationery.com.ph/collections/school). In the backend, `catalog-seed.json` records the original product page for each `sm-*.webp` asset. The top-left retailer mark is cropped out of the catalog photos. Product inventory is maintained separately by ShopEase.

The photographs listed below are from Pexels and are used under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

`stationery-editorial.webp` and `stationery-editorial-small.webp` are the ShopEase storefront artwork.
